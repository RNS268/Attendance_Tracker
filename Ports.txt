# ESP32-CAM PORT CONNECTIONS

This file documents the current and suggested pin connections for the ESP32-CAM Attendance System.

## Current Connections (as defined in code)

| Component      | ESP32-CAM Pin| Type   | Notes                         |
|----------------|--------------|--------|-------------------------------|
| Trigger Button | GPIO 13      | Input  | Pull-up (Active LOW)          |
| Success LED    | GPIO 2       | Output | Green LED (Active HIGH)       |
| Red LED        | GPIO 12      | Output | External Red LED (Active HIGH)|
| Blue LED       | GPIO 3       | Output | Blue LED (Active HIGH)        |
| Status/Process | GPIO 33      | Output | Back LED (Active LOW)         |
| **RESERVED**   | **GPIO 16**  | MEM    |REQUIRED FOR PSRAM - DO NOT USE|
| I2C LCD SDA    | GPIO 14      | I/O    | Connect to LCD SDA            |
| I2C LCD SCL    | GPIO 15      | Output | Connect to LCD SCL            |

## Power Requirements

| LCD Pin | ESP32-CAM Pin| Notes             |
|---------|--------------|-------------------|
| VCC     | 5V           | LCD requires 5V   |
| GND     | GND          | Common Ground     |

---

## Pin Usage Notes

1. **LCD (I2C)**: Uses GPIO 14 (SDA) and GPIO 15 (SCL). These pins are shared with the SD Card interface. Since SD is not used, they are dedicated to the LCD.
2. **Feedback**: The onboard red LED (GPIO 33) blinks 1 time for "Recognized" and 3 times for "Unknown".

---

## IMPORTANT WARNINGS

1. **Power Supply**: The ESP32-CAM is very sensitive to power. When using an LCD and LEDs, ensure you provide at least **2A @ 5V** via the 5V pin.
2. **Booting**: GPIO 0 must be open or HIGH for normal boot. It is used as the XCLK for the camera, so do NOT use it for other components.
3. **Flash Pin**: GPIO 4 is connected to the powerful front-facing flash LED. If you use this pin for a failure LED or buzzer, the flash will also trigger.
4. **SD Card**: If you decide to use an SD card later, GPIOs 2, 4, 12, 13, 14, and 15 will be occupied by the SD MMC interface.
